## Epistemic convention

The corpus distinguishes **Established**, **Working**, **Exploratory**, **Open**, **Deferred** and **Rejected**. Checkpoint v0.1 is intentionally pre-ontology: candidate primitives are not silently treated as final.

## Known deliberate gaps / future stress tests

Health, relationships, bureaucracy, travel, recurring maintenance/routines, `Area`, `Artifact`, recurrence/cadence, subjective experience and collaborative ownership/permissions remain underexplored. They are not missing accidentally; they are preserved as second-wave ontology tests.
