# Life OS - Checkpoint Manifest v0.1

**Purpose:** define the document contract for the first Life OS checkpoint: file set, reading modes, canonical ownership, epistemic rules and generation order.

## Corpus v0.1

### 00 - Re-entry Guide
Fast resume after weeks/months. Answers what Life OS is, why it exists, key principles/decisions, current uncertainty, frontier and next reading step. It does not contain deep specifications or long rationales.

### 01 - Life OS Overview
Whole-system synthesis: context, original problem, responsibilities, philosophy, high-level model, information structure, intentionality/progress, time/experience, ceremonies, self/values, views/intelligence, feedback loops, cases, ontological direction and current frontier.

### 02 - Context, Responsibilities & Design Principles
Foundation/requirements document. Canonical home for personal/project context, problem landscape, SRxx system responsibilities, DPxx principles, boundaries and evaluation criteria. It describes what must be true, not how the ontology/database/UI implements it.

### 03 - Information & Knowledge Model
Core conceptual architecture for loose information, Property/Literal, structured values, Entity, Relation, relation metadata, promotion, Concept, Knowledge, Resource, generic-vs-contextual knowledge, derivation, emergent schema and schema->capability->operation.

### 04 - Intentionality & Progress Model
Idea, Exploration, Commitment, Project, Goal, Scenario, Milestone, Task, Trajectory, Phase, progressive decomposition, distance to action, progress, parking/inactivity and user-controlled Idea->Project promotion.

### 05 - Time, Experience & Practice Model
Time, Session, Temporal Period, Phase relation to time, Metric, Observation, subjective experience, Insight, Practice, activity outputs, aggregation, trend and derived analysis. Preserves Session != Temporal Period.

### 06 - Planning & Ceremonies Model
Operating rhythm: annual/semiannual, monthly/bimonthly, weekly and daily ceremonies, strictness/flexibility, context presentation, decisions, commitment, planning periods, allocations, rails, slot compatibility, planned-vs-actual, retrospective and life maintenance.

### 07 - Self, Values & Resonance Model
Why/how to model Self lightly: revisable self-model, values, interests, deferred Passion, resonance, current direction, autonomy, creativity, understanding, growth, connection, impact, participation, Being<->Acting and declared-self vs observed behavior.

### 08 - Views & System Intelligence Model
Views/lenses plus derived signals. Distinguishes Signal from View and Suggestion from Decision. Covers focus width, temporal distance, modes, inactivity/resume, emerging importance, imbalance/debt, consolidation, commitment mismatch, value alignment and synergies.

### 09 - Case Studies
Single stress-test corpus with Solducci, LaGenteStrana, Agrivoltaico, HistoryExplorer, Speedcubing and Crochet. Each preserves natural behavior, original friction, desired support, involved information/processes, concepts discovered, deliberate non-modeling and ontology questions. Future cases: health, relationships, bureaucracy, travel, recurring maintenance.

### 10 - Rationales & Design Evolution
Deep reasoning/genealogy for progressive structuring, relational knowledge, generic/contextual knowledge, Project identity, Session, Phase vs Temporal Period, rails planning, system as mirror, self/values/resonance, capability model, derivation and views as projections.

### 11 - Decision Register & Open Questions
Governance/evolution: Established decisions, Deferred/Rejected concepts, Working hypotheses and Open questions grouped by primitive ontology, capabilities, knowledge, time, intentionality, self, views/intelligence and implementation.

### 12 - Ontology v0.1 Entry Point
Bridge to the next cycle: candidate primitives/compositions, ambiguities, Entity questions, Property vs Relation, Literal/raw content, relation metadata, promotion, capability semantics, Time, derivation, concepts/cases to stress-test, recommended investigation order and success criteria.

## Canonical Vocabulary Appendix

Maintained primarily through the Overview and model documents until Ontology v0.1 stabilizes: Entity, Property, Literal, Relation, Concept, Knowledge, Resource, Idea, Project, Goal, Scenario, Milestone, Task, Session, Temporal Period, Phase, Metric, Observation, Insight, Practice, Exploration, Retrospective, Ceremony, Commitment, View, Signal, Capability, Operation and Resonance.

## Traceability contract

- **Requirement traceability:** Need/SR -> Model -> Mechanism.
- **Provenance:** Case Study -> Insight -> Rationale -> Decision.
- **Principle traceability:** DP -> concepts/mechanisms governed.

## Generation order

### Wave 1 - Foundations
02 Foundation -> 09 Case Studies -> 11 Decision Register/Open Questions.

### Wave 2 - Models
03 Information/Knowledge -> 04 Intentionality/Progress -> 05 Time/Experience/Practice -> 06 Planning/Ceremonies -> 07 Self/Values/Resonance -> 08 Views/Intelligence.

### Wave 3 - Synthesis
10 Rationales -> 01 Overview -> 00 Re-entry -> 12 Ontology Entry Point.

## Canonical ownership rule

Every important fact has one primary home; other documents summarize, contextualize or reference it. Example: `Phase != Temporal Period` is a canonical decision in 11, defined/interpreted in 04/05, reasoned in 10, grounded in cases and summarized in 01.

## Epistemic rule

Every significant design assertion must remain distinguishable as **Established**, **Working**, **Exploratory**, **Open**, **Deferred** or **Rejected**.

## Evolution rule

Checkpoint v0.1 preserves the pre-ontology state. Later versions must not retroactively rewrite v0.1 to make future conclusions appear inevitable; they may state how a Working/Open item changed status.
